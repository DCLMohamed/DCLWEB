About DCL

Coating steel in Dubai since 1994, for the owners with the least tolerance for a coating that fails early.

The company

Three decades on the region's hardest steel

DCL's leading team coated ships in Singapore yards from 1979 before establishing the company in Dubai in 1994. Since then we have helped Drydocks World Dubai grow into one of the region's leading shipbuilding yards, and become the blasting and coating contractor of choice for some of the world's most demanding owners.

The work spans vessel internal tank coatings, new build, Navy and Oil & Gas projects, FSO / FPSO and FLNG conversions, and tank farm refurbishment. Behind it sits equipment held in enough depth to run several projects at once, and crews who work to written procedures rather than habit.

  • Programmes planned around your shutdown window, not ours.
  • Surface preparation, coating and inspection under one contract.
  • Equipment owned and maintained in house, not sub-hired at short notice.

What we do

Services

Nine core services, delivered as a single scope or packaged together where the specification calls for a full protective system.

Equipment rental

Compressors, blasting machines, airless spray pumps and dehumidification units from our own fleet, maintained in house and delivered to your site.

Abrasive blasting

Open and enclosed blasting to the cleanliness and profile your specification demands, with capacity beyond 100,000 m² per month.

Industrial painting

Airless spray application of primers, intermediates and topcoats, controlled by wet and dry film thickness readings at each stage.

Corrosion protection

Anti-corrosion systems specified for the exposure: splash zone, buried, immersed, insulated or high-temperature service.

Surface preparation

Degreasing, hand and power tool cleaning, hydro-blasting and abrasive blasting to achieve the anchor profile the coating needs.

On-site & off-site coating

Mobilised crews, containment and plant at your facility, or full receipt-to-despatch handling through our workshop.

Tank & vessel linings

Internal tank and vessel coatings applied under controlled conditions, with confined space procedures and full holiday testing.

Passive fire protection

Intumescent and cementitious PFP applied to structural steel and vessels, to the fire rating stated in the project specification.

Dehumidification

Climate control that holds humidity and dew point inside the coating window, using units we designed and built for this region.

Where we work

On site and off site

Some assets cannot move. Others are cheaper and cleaner to treat under a roof. We are set up for both, and will tell you which one suits your programme.

On site

We come to the asset

Crews, compressors, containment and dehumidification mobilised to your plant, yard, jetty or vessel, working around live operations and shutdown windows.

  • Containment and encapsulation to control emissions
  • Confined space and working at height procedures
  • Night and shutdown shifts where the programme requires it

Off site

The asset comes to us

Fabricated steel, pipe spools, valves and components handled through our workshop, where conditions are controlled and throughput is predictable.

  • Controlled blast and paint bays
  • Receipt, protection, storage and despatch

Send us the specification

Drawings, coating schedule, location and target dates are enough for us to come back with a price and a programme.

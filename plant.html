Owned, not hired: the fleet that keeps a blasted surface coatable through a Gulf summer.

Plant & equipment

Humidity is the variable that ruins coatings

One of the most important aspects of any abrasive blasting project is keeping humidity low. DCL designed and fabricated multiple 18,000 m³/hr dehumidification units capable of operating in the harsh regional climate.

Owning that plant, rather than hiring it, is what lets us hold a blasted surface in a coatable condition through a Gulf summer, and run several projects at the same time without robbing one job to serve another.

  • 30 air compressors and 15 dehumidifiers, owned and maintained in house
  • 24 blasting machines, 40 vacuum machines and 16 airless spray pumps
  • Full QC instrument fleet: DFT and surface profile gauges, holiday detectors, gas monitors

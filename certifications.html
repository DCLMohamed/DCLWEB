Quality & HSE

Evidence, not assurances

DCL combines the experience and capability to run large scopes with the safety and quality performance that lets a client sign them off. We work toward zero harm to people and zero damage to property and environment.

  • AMPP-certified coating inspectors on quality hold points
  • NEBOSH IGC health and safety officers on site
  • Ambient, dew point and film thickness readings recorded at hold points
  • Internal training programme for blasting, spray application and confined space work
  • Handover dossier issued on completion
